Data & methodology

Facility sources: OpenStreetMap via Overpass API.

Routing: OSRM driving route service when available. Fallback estimates use straight-line distance and the selected average speed.

Population: EA GeoJSON population attributes. The population total is the sum of the selected field for currently visible EAs.

Accessibility: Distance-circle results are geographic screening estimates. They do not prove road access, facility capacity, service quality or operational status.

Limitations: OpenStreetMap coverage may be incomplete. Validate facility inventories and population attributes against authoritative GBoS and health-sector records before operational use.
